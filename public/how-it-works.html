How it works

1. Create a free account

Sign up with your phone number and email, and confirm the code we send you. You must be 18 or older.

2. Pick a game

Chess, Checkers, Ludo, Tic-Tac-Toe, Connect Four, Reversi and Gomoku. Play a free table to practise, or join a staked table.

3. Deposit with M-Pesa

Enter an amount in the Wallet tab. An M-Pesa prompt arrives on your phone; enter your PIN and your balance updates by itself.

4. Play and win

Each player's stake is held until the game ends. The winner receives the pot minus the platform fee shown on the table. A draw refunds everyone. Leaving a game, losing your connection for over 60 seconds, or running out of time forfeits it.

5. Withdraw to M-Pesa

Request a withdrawal in the Wallet tab. It is paid to your own M-Pesa number, usually within minutes. Larger amounts may be reviewed first.

Game rules in short

  • Connect Four: drop discs into a 7×6 grid; first to connect four in any direction wins.
  • Reversi: place a disc so it traps opposing discs in a straight line and flips them. If you cannot move, your turn is skipped. Most discs wins.
  • Gomoku: take turns on a 15×15 board; first to make five or more in a row wins.

Play responsibly

Only stake money you can afford to lose. See Responsible play for limits and help.
