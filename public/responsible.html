Responsible Play

GameArena.

Play for entertainment, never to solve money problems. Only stake what you can afford to lose.

Tools in your account

Set a daily deposit limit, or take a break from 1 day to 6 months with self-exclusion. A break cannot be undone until it ends, and you can still withdraw your balance.

Warning signs

Chasing losses, hiding your play from family, borrowing to play, or feeling anxious when you are not playing.
